How to read this, and limits

  • Surface temperature is how hot the ground and rooftops are, not the air temperature people feel.
  • Landsat 9 passes at about 10:30 am Dubai time, once every 16 days, so each value is a morning snapshot. Between passes the numbers don't change.
  • The gap is usually larger in summer than in winter. Look at the season buttons above the chart.
  • Scenes where the reference area read far too cold in summer (likely haze) are removed automatically. Cloudy pixels are masked out.
  • Landsat's thermal sensor sees about 100 m squares, resampled to 30 m. Each study area is about 0.25 km².
  • Area R is a nearby comparison area with more vegetation, not a pure rural baseline.
Data: USGS Landsat 9 Collection 2 Level 2 (band ST_B10), ESA Sentinel-2 (NDVI). Processed with Google Earth Engine.
